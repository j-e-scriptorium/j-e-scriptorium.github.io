Various Asides: Essays in Honor of Jørgen Agosto Comgall

edited by Ludovico Ambrosius

Five years after the disappearance of their colleague Jørgen Agosto Comgall, the faculty of the University of St. Isidore have, at editor Ludovico Ambrosius's urging, broken their habit of publishing only monographs to assemble a commemorative volume of some thirty essays.
