# [poems-handturned/notes] Notes
From: Poems Handturned, Machine-Tooled (2024), by Ludovico Ambrosius
URL: https://j-e-scriptorium.github.io/poems-handturned.html#notes

¶1 The front cover illustration derives from M.C. Escher's 1933 print *Phosphorescent Sea*. The back
cover illustration was generated by Stable Diffusion in 2023. A Penrose tiling covers the whole.

¶2 The title of "Rage! Blow!" comes from *King Lear*.

¶3 The apiology in "Rebellion to Tyrants Be Obedience to God" is as accurate as I could make it, but
errors are inevitable. For further remarks on this topic see my Introduction to *O Honeybees: An
Illustrated Anthology of Bee Pomes* (2023).

¶4 "The Singer and the Song" is for MCS.

¶5 The title of "Tinbone! Thinbone!" comes from *The Lord of the Rings*.

¶6 "Apnea" is for ELS.

¶7 "Curtis Yarvin on the Streets of Old New Haven" features JWA.

¶8 The diptych "Brainworms I: ZIZ (November 2019)" and "Brainworms II: DVD (March 2023)" are for
DSS.

¶9 "March 29, 2023: Damasked Eliezer Arrives in Time" transposes into verse Eliezer Yudkowsky's
*TIME* op-ed of that date.

¶10 "Our Word / Its Bond" had assistance from NMW.

¶11 "Curtis Yarvin at an Undisclosed Location" features NRH.

¶12 "Rent, Sodomy, and the Code: Or, The Shrunken Head of Jeremy Bentham, Esq." repurposes passages
from Jeremy Bentham's essays "In Defense of Usury" and "On Paederasty."

¶13 "Above the Fray" commemorates CK, J.

¶14 "King Solomon's Mines" is for SMS. The accompanying illustrations are stills from the 1950 MGM
movie *King Solomon's Mines*.
