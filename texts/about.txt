# About the St. Isidore University Press
From: Jsidoris Euniversitatis Scriptorium
URL: https://j-e-scriptorium.github.io/about.html

¶1 St. Isidore University Press, formally **Jsidoris Euniversitatis Scriptorium** ("JES" for short),
is a project of the faculty of St. Isidore University, located in far northern California, although
not to be found on most maps. The editorial board of JES includes the following:

¶2 - Ludovico Ambrosius is professor of bee-keeping and board game design.

¶3 - Jørgen Agosto Comgall is professor of the philosophy of the history of philosophy of history.

¶4 - E. Lawrence Hayward is instructor of regularity.

¶5 - Lyzander Keretzky is lecturer in noneuclidean architecture.

¶6 - Edkar Marenko is instructor of industry.

¶7 - Porphyry Pterpsichore is professor of order and color.

¶8 - Hugo Purcell is professor of psychometry and physiology.

¶9 - Michael A. Seeley is lecturer in systemic history.

¶10 - Tom G. Turner is professor of dialects and dialectics.

¶11 The pseudonym of the chief clerk of JES is Salvator R. Tarnmoor. He sporadically checks his
email address, but a more reliable point of contact would be @s_r_tarnmoor on X.
