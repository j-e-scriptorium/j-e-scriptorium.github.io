# [coke-reports-verse/reports] The Reports
From: The Reports of Sir Edward Coke, Kt. In Verse (1742; digitized edition 2026), by an anonymous
  18th c. poet, edited by A. Clanker as directed by E. Lawrence Hayward
URL: https://j-e-scriptorium.github.io/coke-reports-verse.html#reports

¶1 Part I

¶2     **I** (#part-I-I)
    [fol. 1] **Buckhurst**, Such deeds as warranty deraign,
    The feoffee if he warrant may detain.

¶3     **II** (#part-I-II)
    [fol. 14] **Pelham**, 'Tis forfeiture if as vouchee,
    Tenant for life suffers recovery.

¶4     **III** (#part-I-III)
    [fol. 22] **Porter**, The mortmain statutes do not bar
    Such uses, as not superstitious are.

¶5     **IV** (#part-I-IV)
    [fol. 40] **Alton Wood**, Where the king grants 'tis not good
    If double, or by him not understood.

¶6     **V** (#part-I-V)
    [fol. 61] **Capel**, Recov'ry 'gainst him had in tail:
    Charge of reversion and remainder fail.

¶7     **VI** (#part-I-VI)
    [fol. 66] **Archer**, If he for life infeoff in fee:
    It bars remainders in contingency.

¶8     **VII** (#part-I-VII)
    [fol. 76] **Bredon**, It is not forfeiture in fine:
    If he for life with tail remainder join.

¶9     **VIII** (#part-I-VIII)
    [fol. 83] **Corbet**, Proviso tenant doing ought
    To bar the tail, the 'state to lease is naught.

¶10     **IX** (#part-I-IX)
    [fol. 93] **Shelley**, Where ancestors a freehold take:
    The words (his heirs) a limitacion make.

¶11     **X** (#part-I-X)
    [fol. 110] **Albany**, Who power to revoke enjoys:
    If he a Feoffment makes, that pow'r destroys.

¶12     **XI** (#part-I-XI)
    [fol. 120] **Chudleigh**, Feoffees holding for life, they may
    To heir (ere use contingent takes) convey.

¶13     **XII** (#part-I-XII)
    [fol. 147] **Mayowe**, Feoffment conditional, feoffee
    Grants rent good, tho' condition broken be.

¶14     **XIII** (#part-I-XIII)
    [fol. 153] **Chedington**, Term for years, if lessee live
    So long, the residue lessor can't give.

¶15     **XIV** (#part-I-XIV)
    [fol. 173] **Diggs**, A revoking power by deed inroll'd,
    By fine before inrolment is controul'd.

¶16     **XV** (#part-I-XV)
    [fol. 175] **Mildmay**, By cov'nant seis'd t'advance his Blood:
    Proviso gene'ral (he may lease) not good.

¶17 Part II

¶18     **I** (#part-II-I)
    [fol. 3] **Anser**, On condition to make a deed:
    Unlettered if he seal, some must it read.

¶19     **II** (#part-II-II)
    [fol. 4] **Goddard**, Th' effect the deed doth take shall be
    Not from the date, but the delivery.

¶20     **III** (#part-II-III)
    [fol. 9] **Thoroughgood**, If a deed be read amiss
    To one unlettered, voidable it is.

¶21     **IV** (#part-II-IV)
    [fol. 15] **Wiseman**, Remainder to the queen does fail:
    Against recovery, by him in tail.

¶22     **V** (#part-II-V)
    [fol. 16] **Lane**, Copyholder who accepts a lease,
    Of the same land, his copyhold doth cease.

¶23     **VI** (#part-II-VI)
    [fol. 23] **Baldwin**, A lease to one and to his heirs,
    For years with seisin, yet but lease for years.

¶24     **VII** (#part-II-VII)
    [fol. 25] **Bankrupt**, One debtor pays with Goods, yet those
    Commissioners may to the rest dispose.

¶25     **VIII** (#part-II-VIII)
    [fol. 31] **Bettisworth**, Liv'ry on the land is ill:
    If termor in the house continue still.

¶26     **IX** (#part-II-IX)
    [fol. 32] **Doddington**, Gen'ral grant is void, if where
    The land doth lie, the place mistaken were.

¶27     **X** (#part-II-X)
    [fol. 35] **Hayward**, Grantee may his election make,
    By bargain, sale, or common law to take.

¶28     **XI** (#part-II-XI)
    [fol. 43] **Winchester**, Clergymen they're not to pay:
    But tithes prescribe, and so their lessees may.

¶29     **XII** (#part-II-XII)
    [fol. 46] **Canterbury**, Thirty-first Hen'ry eight
    Religious houses meant not, act more late.
    A lawful possession in unity:
    To discharge tithes, it must perpetual be.

¶30     **XIII** (#part-II-XIII)
    [fol. 50] **Cholmely**, Remainder grant for life of Tail
    To king remainder, such remainder fail.

¶31     **XIV** (#part-II-XIV)
    [fol. 55] **Buckler**, A lease to have from future day
    For life, void; nor attornment help it may.

¶32     **XV** (#part-II-XV)
    [fol. 56] **Beckwith**, Both join in fine of the wife's land
    With sev'ral uses, neither use shall stand.

¶33     **XVI** (#part-II-XVI)
    [fol. 59] **Winnington**, Feoffor ent'ring ere feoffee
    Refeoffs and leases must disseisor be.

¶34     **XVII** (#part-II-XVII)
    [fol. 60] **Wiscot**, If the reversion unto one
    Jointenant granted be, the jointure's gone.

¶35     **XVIII** (#part-II-XVIII)
    [fol. 66] **Tooker**, Jointenant he attorns to vest
    The whole reversion, good without the rest.

¶36     **XIX** (#part-II-XIX)
    [fol. 69] **Cromwell**, Proviso fits condition well.
    Sole standing, feoffor's word if it compel,
    Tho' an estate extinct be, by fine past
    Condition yet annext thereto may last.

¶37     **XX** (#part-II-XX)
    [fol. 91] **Bingham**, Where heir tail the reverting fee
    Expectant hath, he not in ward shall be.

¶38 Part III

¶39     **I** (#part-III-I)
    [fol. 1] **Winchester**, Gen'ral words no deposition
    By act to king of action or condition
    Error cannot be brought by him in fee,
    Living remainder, to reverse recov'ry.

¶40     **II** (#part-III-II)
    [fol. 5] **Cuppledike**, Jointenant wife, husband alone
    Comes in vouchee, remainder in tail's gone.

¶41     **III** (#part-III-III)
    [fol. 7] **Heydon**, To copyhold, reach gen'ral words
    Of act, where harm to tenant none; nor lords.

¶42     **IV** (#part-III-IV)
    [fol. 9] **Dowty**, In king lands vest not, of disseised
    Traytor, till *scire facias* or till seis'd.

¶43     **V** (#part-III-V)
    [fol. 11] **Herbert**, A judgment special and extent
    'Gainst heir only of lands come by descent.

¶44     **VI** (#part-III-VI)
    [fol. 19] **Boraston**, Till at age the son arrive,
    Devise the whole time's good, he not alive.

¶45     **VII** (#part-III-VII)
    [fol. 22] **Walker**, The lessor from lessee for years
    After assignment due may have arrears.

¶46     **VIII** (#part-III-VIII)
    [fol. 25] **Butler**, *In pais* refusal shall enure
    To divest jointure made since coverture.

¶47     **IX** (#part-III-IX)
    [fol. 37] **Radcliffe**, By fifth of Mary, Mother may
    Enter, if any takes her ward away.

¶48     **X** (#part-III-X)
    [fol. 47] **Boyton**, When Shrieve in execution takes,
    And keeps in other county, 'cape this makes.

¶49     **XI** (#part-III-XI)
    [fol. 50] **Brown**, If feme, tenant in tail special let
    For lives her 'state, the issue may defeat.

¶50     **XII** (#part-III-XII)
    [fol. 52] **Ridgway**, 'Ere action brought if pris'ner be
    Retaken in fresh suit, the Sheriff's free.

¶51     **XIII** (#part-III-XIII)
    [fol. 58] **Lincoln**, Remainder barr'd, by feme's warranty
    Collat'ral to baron's, makes good recov'ry.

¶52     **XIV** (#part-III-XIV)
    [fol. 64] **Pennant**, Condition broken, rent ta'en is
    Due since the notice, lease not good by this.

¶53     **XV** (#part-III-XV)
    [fol. 71] **Westby**, In Execution charg'd by two,
    'Scape, if old sheriff gives but one to th' new.

¶54     **XVI** (#part-III-XVI)
    [fol. 73] **Norwich**, To dean and chapter good translation
    By Edward sixth not naming the foundation.

¶55     **XVII** (#part-III-XVII)
    [fol. 77] **Fermor**, If lessee copyholder pass
    Fine, as to lord the 'state is as it was.

¶56     **XVIII** (#part-III-XVIII)
    [fol. 80] **Twine**, General gift with hanging secrecy
    Trust giver still possest, covin imply.

¶57     **XIX** (#part-III-XIX)
    [fol. 84] **Fine**, He in Remainder tail dies, his heir
    Beyond sea, proclamations past, they bar.

¶58 Part IV

¶59     **I** (#part-IV-I)
    [fol. 1] **Vernon**, Dower cannot be claim'd by wife
    Who enters on remainder for her life.

¶60     **II** (#part-IV-II)
    [fol. 8] **Bevill**, Seisin of higher services
    By lord, seisin of all inferior is.

¶61     **III — Actions for slander** (#part-IV-III)
    [fol. 12] **Cromwell**, The law which doth a pain enact
    For slander of a peer's a general act.
    [fol. 14] **Cutler**, For scand'lous articles to tie
    To good behaviour, action will not lie.
    [fol. 15] **Stanhope**, Forsworne is but a hasty word
    Unless he add (before judge of record).
    [fol. 15] **Hext**, Action lies when e'er the words are such
    As they his life on whom they're spoke may touch.
    [fol. 16] **Byrchley**, That he's corrupt, will Action bear
    Discoursing of the Place of Officer.
    [fol. 16] **Stuckley**, Justice of Peace, if any saith
    That he hides felons, a good action li'th.
    [fol. 16] **Snag**, If a person says he kill'd my wife,
    No action lies, if she be yet alive.
    [fol. 16] **Davis**, For slander action will not lie,
    Unless some temp'ral loss incur thereby.
    [fol. 17] **James**, If a certain person is not laid
    And matter *innuendo* will not aid.
    [fol. 18] **Oxford**, If in London an action's had
    For calling Whore, by law that custom's bad.
    [fol. 18] **Gerard**, Who e'er doth publish knowingly
    A forged title, action well does lie.
    [fol. 18] **Brittridge**, Words adjective will action bear,
    If slander in the subsequent appear.
    [fol. 20] **Barham**, Where words will yield a milder sense,
    An *innuendo* shall not make th' offence.

¶62     **IV — Copyhold Cases** (#part-IV-IV)
    [fol. 21] **Browne**, Copyhold *possessio fratris* lies
    The eldest son before admittance dies.
    [fol. 22] **Rivett**, No curtesy of copy lands,
    Unless a special custom so commands.
    [fol. 23] **Deale**, Where a copy tail'd, recovery
    By plaint a discontinuance wou'd be.
    [fol. 23] **Bullock**, No forfeiture if seis'd for life
    Husband the land surrender of his wife.
    [fol. 23] **Foxton**, Descent of copyhold estate
    A right of entry doth not abogate.
    [fol. 23] **Clarke**, Where surrender made to use of will,
    Inheritance is in the tenant still:
    If the lord grant a copyhold and dies;
    No dower to's wife, shall out of't arise.
    [fol. 24] **Rous**, He that holds at suff'rance may admit,
    But if he grant, the lord may vacate it.
    [fol. 24] **Murrel**, If lord the fee and copy sever,
    Fine gone, but rent and services last ever.
    [fol. 25] **Kite**, An admittance is of no effect,
    If the surrender do it not direct.
    [fol. 26] **Melwich**, Although the steward cannot, yet
    The lord out of the Manor may, admit.
    [fol. 27] **Clifton**, No act of stranger can enure
    As of wife's copyhold to make forf'ture.
    [fol. 27] **Taverner**, Many copyholds in one
    Manor, one forfeited, the rest not gone.
    [fol. 27] **Hubbard**, If lord impose excessive fine,
    The tenant safely payment may decline.
    [fol. 29] **Bunting**, Surrender unlimited's for life,
    Husband surrender may to the use of wife.
    [fol. 29] **Down**, Where for life by custom grant is good,
    There grant is so during her widowhood.
    [fol. 29] **Shaw**, Wife of copyholder not endow'd
    But where by special custom so allow'd.
    [fol. 30] **Hoe**, If a custom be, that underwood
    Of copyhold is grantable, 'tis good.
    [fol. 31] **Frenche**, By any lawful interruption
    Of custom, not by wrongful, copy's gone.

¶63     **V** (#part-IV-V)
    [fol. 32] **Mitton**, Of county-court, the clerkship none
    Can grant, or keep the gaol but shrieve alone.

¶64     **VI** (#part-IV-VI)
    [fol. 35] **Bozoun**, If void the grant of king be found,
    The *non obstante* will not make it sound.

¶65     **VII** (#part-IV-VII)
    [fol. 36] **Tyrringham**, No prescription can extend
    Common on ought but tillage to append.

¶66     **VIII — Appeals and Indictments** (#part-IV-VIII)
    [fol. 39] **Vaux**, None convict upon appeal shall be
    Indicted for the self same felony.
    [fol. 40] **Wetherel**, In appeal of homicide
    Convict t'indict of murder is deny'd.
    [fol. 40] **Young**, An assistant or the officer
    To kill is murder, though no malice were.
    [fol. 41] **Walker**, Indictment shall not harmed be
    By surplusage, if no repugnancy.
    [fol. 42] **Hume**, In murder must the indictment lie
    Exactly at the place were he did die.
    [fol. 43] **Hudson**, That he recover'd of the same
    In battery bars an appeal of mayhem.
    [fol. 43] **Syer**, Principal, pardon or clergy have
    Before th' attaint, the accessary save.
    [fol. 44] **Vaux**, *Auterfoits acquits* or convicts are
    Not to Indictment unless legal bar.
    [fol. 47] **Wait**, But one appeal may be against all
    The accessaries and the principal.
    [fol. 47] **Hill**, It's no policy, if you indict,
    To recite statutes, lest you misrecite.

¶67     **IX** (#part-IV-IX)
    [fol. 48] **Ognel**, For rent exec'tors of grantee
    May 'straine who ere claims from grantee's feoffee.

¶68     **X** (#part-IV-X)
    [fol. 52] **Rawlyn**, To him in fee indented lease
    For years estoppel with the term shall cease.

¶69     **XI** (#part-IV-XI)
    [fol. 54] **Sadlers**, If heirless the king's tenant die,
    The freehold's in the king immediately.

¶70     **XII** (#part-IV-XII)
    [fol. 60] **Force**, If the devisee a woman takes
    And covert dies, a countermand this makes.

¶71     **XIII** (#part-IV-XIII)
    [fol. 62] **Harlakendon**, Excepted wood since leas'd
    The land lessee, a distinct interest.

¶72     **XIV** (#part-IV-XIV)
    [fol. 64] **Fulwood**, Recognizance to chamberlain
    Of London shall to successors remain.

¶73     **XV** (#part-IV-XV)
    [fol. 70] **Hynde**, First by fine reversion then is sold
    By deed he's in before the deed inroll'd.

¶74     **XVI** (#part-IV-XVI)
    [fol. 72] **Boroughes**, Of rent reserved the demand,
    No place assigned, must be upon the land.

¶75     **XVII** (#part-IV-XVII)
    [fol. 74] **Palmer**, If shrieve, term by *elegit* sell
    Not years behind reciting, 'tis not well.

¶76     **XVIII** (#part-IV-XVIII)
    [fol. 75] **Holland**, Of statutes which extend to all,
    The Court must notice take as general.

¶77     **XIX** (#part-IV-XIX)
    [fol. 77] **Corporation**, Election well may be
    By common council of commonalty.

¶78     **XX** (#part-IV-XX)
    [fol. 78] **Digby**, By the second institution,
    Though ere induct dispenst, first living's gone.

¶79     **XXI** (#part-IV-XXI)
    [fol. 80] **Nokes**, General covenant by law impli'd,
    By covenant express is qualifi'd.

¶80     **XXII** (#part-IV-XXII)
    [fol. 81] **Corbet**, Devise of land till a sum rais'd,
    The profits from such time shall be apprais'd.

¶81     **XXIII** (#part-IV-XXIII)
    [fol. 83] **Southcote**, General bailment, robbery
    Is not in special or in pawn good plea.

¶82     **XXIV** (#part-IV-XXIV)
    [fol. 86] **Luttrell**, The owners 'change a fulling mill,
    To one for corn, prescription lasteth still.

¶83     **XXV** (#part-IV-XXV)
    [fol. 89] **Drury**, If countess chaplains three retain,
    The last a Dispensation cannot gain.

¶84     **XXVI** (#part-IV-XXVI)
    [fol. 92] **Slade**, For corn sold the vendor well may chuse
    Action of debt, or on the case may use.

¶85     **XXVII** (#part-IV-XXVII)
    [fol. 104] **Adams**, T' use superstitious who bestows
    Land, though on his own blood, to king it goes.

¶86     **XXVIII** (#part-IV-XXVIII)
    [fol. 117] **Acton**, Feme (chaplain takes) of noble blood,
    Then marries, yet his dispensation's good.

¶87     **XXIX** (#part-IV-XXIX)
    [fol. 119] **Dumpor**, Condition lessee alien not,
    Nor without leave assigns, gone leave once got.

¶88     **XXX** (#part-IV-XXX)
    [fol. 121] **Bustard**, Exchange consists in privity,
    And is defeat, if part evicted be.

¶89     **XXXI** (#part-IV-XXXI)
    [fol. 123] **Beverley**, Acts of one non compos are
    Voidable by executor or heir.

¶90 Part V

¶91     **I** (#part-V-I)
    [fol. 1] **Caudrey**, 'Gainst common prayer if parson say
    In sermon ought, bishop deprive him may.

¶92     **II — Leases** (#part-V-II)
    [fol. *1] **Clayton**, If lease from henceforth do begin,
    Day of delivery is taken in.

¶93     **III** (#part-V-III)
    [fol. *2] **Elmer**, If bishop oust lessee for years,
    And then lease for three lives, this void appears.

¶94     **IV** (#part-V-IV)
    [fol. *3] **Jewel**, A lease of fair reserving rent,
    To be within the first of queen not meant.

¶95     **V** (#part-V-V)
    [fol. *3] **Mountjoy**, Lease prejudiceth heir, if more
    Land let, or rent days fewer than before.

¶96     **VI** (#part-V-VI)
    [fol. *7] **Windham**, Joint words are sev'rally in lease
    Of separate lands, after their terms do cease.

¶97     **VII** (#part-V-VII)
    [fol. *9] **Brudnel**, Administring for minors four,
    When any at full age arrives, gives o're.

¶98     **VIII** (#part-V-VIII)
    [fol. *10] **Henstead**, If lessor or lessee do wed,
    The will is not by this determined.

¶99     **IX** (#part-V-IX)
    [fol. *11] **Ive**, Lessee a future interest takes
    Within his year, this a surrender makes.

¶100     **X** (#part-V-X)
    [fol. *12] **Saunders**, Mines open, lands leas'd do comprise;
    Waste in tenuit against assignee lies.

¶101     **XI** (#part-V-XI)
    [fol. *13] **Rosse**, Lease for life to one and assignee,
    And of two more, good lease for lives of three.

¶102     **XII** (#part-V-XII)
    [fol. *13] **Countess of Salop**, For permissive waste,
    Tenant at will by trespass must be cast.

¶103     **XIII** (#part-V-XIII)
    [fol. *14] **Ecclesiastick's Lease**, though to the king,
    Not good, unless the statute following.

¶104     **XIV — Covenants** (#part-V-XIV)
    [fol. *16] **Spencer**, Upon the land thing new to do,
    Assignee named by covenant is bound to.

¶105     **XV** (#part-V-XV)
    [fol. *18] **Slingsby**, Where cov'nants to two and either,
    They have joint interest, and must sue together.

¶106     **XVI** (#part-V-XVI)
    [fol. *19] **Rosewell**, T'assure as vendees counsel shall
    Devise, vendee himself not meant at all.

¶107     **XVII** (#part-V-XVII)
    [fol. *19] **Higginbottom**, If his counsel do advise
    Under himself to assure, it doth suffice.

¶108     **XVIII** (#part-V-XVIII)
    [fol. *20] **Stiles**, This indenture are not words that make
    A deed indented, if't indenting lack.

¶109     **XIX** (#part-V-XIX)
    [fol. *20] **Main**, On surrender bound new lease to let,
    Fine and lease to another forfeits it.

¶110     **XX** (#part-V-XX)
    [fol. *21] **Laughter**, No breach if of disjunctives, one
    By act of God become not to be done.

¶111     **XXI** (#part-V-XXI)
    [fol. *22] **Halling**, T'assure at cost of cov'nantee,
    The first act must the covenantor's be.

¶112     **XXII** (#part-V-XXII)
    [fol. *22] **Mathewson**, Divers cov'nant in one deed,
    One seal is broke, that party's only freed.

¶113     **XXIII** (#part-V-XXIII)
    [fol. *23] **Lambe**, Who foe'er conditions to assure,
    By advice of Stranger, must th' advice procure.

¶114     **XXIV** (#part-V-XXIV)
    [fol. *24] **Broughton**, If Surety pays upon the day,
    He on his counter-bond recover may.

¶115     **XXV** (#part-V-XXV)
    [fol. *24] **Windsor**, By cov'nant houses to repair
    Assignees, though not named, obliged are.

¶116     **XXVI** (#part-V-XXVI)
    [fol. *24] **Palmer**, If vendee by assignment wou'd
    To take, assignment of his interest's good.

¶117     **XXVII** (#part-V-XXVII)
    [fol. *25] **Rutland**, By two indentures limited
    The uses of a fine, the first shall lead.

¶118     **XXVIII — Executors** (#part-V-XXVIII)
    [fol. *27] **Russel**, To plead in bar release that's made
    By infant and executor, is bad.

¶119     **XXIX** (#part-V-XXIX)
    [fol. *28] **Middleton**, A release e'er proof of will,
    Though he refuse and others prove, not ill.

¶120     **XXX** (#part-V-XXX)
    [fol. *28] **Harrison**, Bond executors must quit
    'Fore statute for a cov'nant unbroke yet.

¶121     **XXXI** (#part-V-XXXI)
    [fol. *29] **Piggot**, For minors who administer,
    That they're within seventeen years aver.

¶122     **XXXII** (#part-V-XXXII)
    [fol. *29] **Prince**, Such administrator doing ought
    To th' minor's prejudice, that act is naught.

¶123     **XXXIII** (#part-V-XXXIII)
    [fol. *30] **Coulter**, Against a creditor 'tis vain,
    If in's own wrong executor retain.

¶124     **XXXIV** (#part-V-XXXIV)
    [fol. *31] **Hargrave**, An executor for the rent due
    In his own time, in *detinet* must sue.

¶125     **XXXV** (#part-V-XXXV)
    [fol. *32] **Pettifer**, *Nulla bona*, waste was made,
    *De bonis propriis*, judgment shall be had.

¶126     **XXXVI** (#part-V-XXXVI)
    [fol. *32] **Robinson**, As administrator, debt
    If barr'd, as executor may sue yet.

¶127     **XXXVII** (#part-V-XXXVII)
    [fol. *33] **Read**, Using Goods t' intestate did belong
    'Fore probate, executor's in's own wrong.

¶128     **XXXVIII — Amendment** (#part-V-XXXVIII)
    [fol. *34] **Plater**, For taking fishes trespass brought,
    Their number and their nature shew he ought.

¶129     **XXXIX** (#part-V-XXXIX)
    [fol. *36] **Walcot**, 'Gainst Wife and husband, for wife's debt
    *Dum sola*, lay debet and *detinet*.

¶130     **XL** (#part-V-XL)
    [fol. 36] **Baynham**, From fewer vills, a visne sent
    Is wrong directed, and insufficient.

¶131     **XLI** (#part-V-XLI)
    [fol. *37] **Gardiner**, False return of Jurors made
    By sheriff, statute of the queen doth aid.

¶132     **XLII** (#part-V-XLII)
    [fol. 37] **Bishop**, Material variance 'twixt the writ
    And count, not any statute helpeth it.

¶133     **XLIII** (#part-V-XLIII)
    [fol. *38] **Tey**, In the grant and render of a fine,
    Error the cognizor shall not assign.

¶134     **XLIV** (#part-V-XLIV)
    [fol. *40] **Dormer**, Though *praecipe* disjunctive be,
    No error in common recovery.

¶135     **XLV** (#part-V-XLV)
    [fol. *41] **Rowland**, Venire, not the name of Shrieve,
    Nor the return indors'd, no acts relieve.

¶136     **XLVI** (#part-V-XLVI)
    [fol. 42] **Rutland**, If *postea* jurors name misprise
    Examin'd, the venire rectifies:
    But if the jurors they mistaken be
    In the venire, there's no remedy.

¶137     **XLVII** (#part-V-XLVII)
