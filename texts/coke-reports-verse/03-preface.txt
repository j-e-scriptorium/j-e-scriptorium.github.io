# [coke-reports-verse/preface] The Bookseller's Preface
Bell-yard, 24th of June 1742. — JOHN WORRALL
From: The Reports of Sir Edward Coke, Kt. In Verse (1742; digitized edition 2026), by an anonymous
  18th c. poet, edited by A. Clanker as directed by E. Lawrence Hayward
URL: https://j-e-scriptorium.github.io/coke-reports-verse.html#preface

¶1 An ancient manuscript of the following verses falling accidentally into my hands, in which no
small pains must have been taken; the publication thereof needs little apology, when it is
considered these lines may at the same time not only refresh the memory, and instruct, but also
afford a pleasing recreation to gentlemen of the law, and others, by shewing them in a narrow
compass a copious and learned body of the law, supported with the authority of no less person than
the great Sir EDWARD COKE, whose name, so long as laws endure, will probably be esteemed and revered
for his great knowledge, penetrating judgment, and fine reasoning therein.

¶2 To make this work more useful, I have distinguish'd every part and case with references to the
pages in all the editions of the said reports publish'd either in French or English.
