Near as We Can Tell: Natural Histories in Verse

by A. Clanker
with an introduction by Ludovico Ambrosius

Sixteen poems on sixteen natural phenomena, each setting out the science and then asking what it might mean, and arranged by the four elements: from the earth at the center outward, sphere by sphere, to the light of the sun.
